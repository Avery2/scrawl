# Scrawl

Show an agent a problem instead of describing it.

Press a shortcut, then talk, click around and draw on the screen. Scrawl records the screen, your voice and your marks, then packs them into a compact bundle: a few annotated keyframes (a contact sheet) and a transcript aligned to what happened when. The bundle goes to OpenCode or the clipboard in place of a screenshot plus a dictated explanation.
