# Capsule motion

Read this before changing the capsule's animation (`src/ui/lab.rs`, `src/ui/motion.rs`, `src/native/`, `perf/pill/`).

## Capsule motion loop (`src/ui/lab.rs`, `src/ui/motion.rs`, `perf/pill/`)

`scrawl lab pill <tap-then-area|tap-then-window|tap-then-screen|hold-switcher|finish> [--repeat 0] [--time-scale 0.25] [--trace f.jsonl]` replays the capsule's states over a plain backdrop with a pretend recording (no capture, mic, hotkeys, ingest or sends) and quits; `--trace` (or `SCRAWL_MOTION_TRACE`) writes every frame's capsule, child boxes (`chip:Area`, `lead`, `close`…), `content.blur/alpha`, `shell.width` and `phase:*` events as motion-lint JSONL.

`perf/pill/record.sh <binary> <dir> [--time-scale 0.25] [scene…]` records each scene (trace + 60 fps screen clip) and runs motion-lint (`perf/pill/motion-lint.config.json`); `bun ~/code/open-source/motion-lint/cli/motion-lint.ts baseline <before> <after> --config perf/pill/motion-lint.config.json` diffs two runs, and `uv run perf/pill/clips.py before.mov after.mov out [--slow 4] [--strip-at s]` stacks labelled before/after clips and a frame strip. Look at the frames too: an invisible capsule has no motion to flag.

Springs and fades live in `motion::Tune`; `scrawl tune list|set <key> <v>|preset <1-12>|reset` edits `~/.config/scrawl/tune.json` (`$SCRAWL_TUNE`), which dev builds, the installed app included, reload within 250 ms, and `scrawl tune rust` prints the values to paste into `Tune::DEFAULT`. One animation clock (`motion::now()`, scaled by `timeScale`); springs advance to now as each capsule frame draws (`step_island`).

The Metal capsule (`src/native/pill/model.rs`) is a true capsule (radius = height / 2) around 32 pt chips inset 4 pt, with no dividers: items in a group sit 2 pt apart and groups (lead | chips | ✕) 6 pt apart. The selected mode is one highlight that slides between chips. Four `Tune` keys pick its options (`Tune::pill_opts`): `pill.origin` is where it enters and leaves from (0 centre scale, 1 drops open from the top edge, the default, 2 unrolls from the left end cap); `pill.anchor` 1 (default) lays the recording capsule's chips out where the picker puts them, so tapping Area or Window moves only the left wall and the highlight, and 0 keeps every state centred; `pill.blur` and `pill.shellBlur` are the content and edge blur on entry and exit (1.5 and 0 pt). Its springs come from the kit-motion palette and are tunable too: `pill.enter.*` (.25/0) and `pill.exit.*` (.2/0), `pill.morph.open.*` / `pill.morph.close.*` (.42/.16 and .38/.08, width only), and `pill.highlight.lead.*` / `pill.highlight.trail.*` (.18/0 and .25/0: the highlight's edge facing the slide leads, so it stretches toward the next chip and settles to its width; a hold-switcher key step is instant, .1/0). `pill.punch` (2 pt) is how far the reveal shoots past rest, on a curve over the zero-bounce entry spring (`punch_curve`); exits never shoot. Presets 7–12 are Centre, Unroll, Centred chips, 0.3.3 (its origin, blur and springs), Drop (zero bounce, no punch) and Punch (4 pt). Outline and rects are snapped edge by edge on a 1/128 pt grid, so an edge that isn't moving can't shimmer. Leaving items fade before a wall reaches them (`COLLAPSE_WAIT_S`, the look-ahead cap, which eases to nothing with the square of the time left); `nothing_vanishes_from_visible_opacity_in_one_frame_or_flickers` checks the last visible step at 60 and 120 Hz.
