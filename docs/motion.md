# Capsule motion

Read this before changing the capsule's animation (`src/ui/lab.rs`, `src/ui/motion.rs`, `src/native/`, `perf/pill/`).

## Capsule motion loop (`src/ui/lab.rs`, `src/ui/motion.rs`, `perf/pill/`)

`scrawl lab pill <tap-then-area|tap-then-window|tap-then-screen|hold-switcher|finish> [--repeat 0] [--time-scale 0.25] [--trace f.jsonl]` replays the capsule's states over a plain backdrop with a pretend recording (no capture, mic, hotkeys, ingest or sends) and quits; `--trace` (or `SCRAWL_MOTION_TRACE`) writes every frame's capsule, child boxes (`chip:Area`, `lead`, `close`…), `content.blur/alpha`, `shell.width` and `phase:*` events as motion-lint JSONL.

`perf/pill/record.sh <binary> <dir> [--time-scale 0.25] [scene…]` records each scene (trace + 60 fps screen clip) and runs motion-lint (`perf/pill/motion-lint.config.json`); `bun ~/code/open-source/motion-lint/cli/motion-lint.ts baseline <before> <after> --config perf/pill/motion-lint.config.json` diffs two runs, and `uv run perf/pill/clips.py before.mov after.mov out [--slow 4] [--strip-at s]` stacks labelled before/after clips and a frame strip. Look at the frames too: an invisible capsule has no motion to flag.

Springs and fades live in `motion::Tune`; `scrawl tune list|set <key> <v>|preset <1-10>|reset` edits `~/.config/scrawl/tune.json` (`$SCRAWL_TUNE`), which dev builds, the installed app included, reload within 250 ms, and `scrawl tune rust` prints the values to paste into `Tune::DEFAULT`. One animation clock (`motion::now()`, scaled by `timeScale`); springs advance to now as each capsule frame draws (`step_island`).

The Metal capsule (`src/native/pill/model.rs`) is a true capsule (radius = height / 2) around 32 pt chips inset 4 pt, with no dividers: items in a group sit 2 pt apart and groups (lead | chips | ✕) 6 pt apart. The selected mode is one highlight that slides between chips. Four `Tune` keys pick its options (`Tune::pill_opts`): `pill.origin` is where it enters and leaves from (0 centre scale, 1 drops open from the top edge, the default, 2 unrolls from the left end cap); `pill.anchor` 1 (default) lays the recording capsule's chips out where the picker puts them, so tapping Area or Window moves only the left wall and the highlight, and 0 keeps every state centred; `pill.blur` and `pill.shellBlur` are the content and edge blur on entry and exit (1.5 and 0 pt). Presets 7–10 are Centre, Unroll, Centred chips and the 0.3.3 blur. Leaving items fade before a wall reaches them (`COLLAPSE_WAIT_S`, the look-ahead cap); `nothing_vanishes_from_visible_opacity_in_one_frame_or_flickers` checks it at 60 and 120 Hz.
