# Capsule motion

Read this before changing the capsule's animation (`src/ui/lab.rs`, `src/ui/motion.rs`, `src/native/`, `perf/pill/`).

## Capsule motion loop (`src/ui/lab.rs`, `src/ui/motion.rs`, `perf/pill/`)

`scrawl lab pill <tap-then-area|tap-then-window|tap-then-screen|hold-switcher|finish> [--repeat 0] [--time-scale 0.25] [--trace f.jsonl]` replays the capsule's states over a plain backdrop with a pretend recording (no capture, mic, hotkeys, ingest or sends) and quits; `--trace` (or `SCRAWL_MOTION_TRACE`) writes every frame's capsule, child boxes (`chip:Area`, `lead`, `close`…), `content.blur/alpha`, `shell.width` and `phase:*` events as motion-lint JSONL.

`perf/pill/record.sh <binary> <dir> [--time-scale 0.25] [scene…]` records each scene (trace + 60 fps screen clip) and runs motion-lint (`perf/pill/motion-lint.config.json`); `bun ~/code/open-source/motion-lint/cli/motion-lint.ts baseline <before> <after> --config perf/pill/motion-lint.config.json` diffs two runs, and `uv run perf/pill/clips.py before.mov after.mov out [--slow 4] [--strip-at s]` stacks labelled before/after clips and a frame strip. Look at the frames too: an invisible capsule has no motion to flag.

Springs and fades live in `motion::Tune`; `scrawl tune list|set <key> <v>|preset <1-12>|reset` edits `~/.config/scrawl/tune.json` (`$SCRAWL_TUNE`), which dev builds, the installed app included, reload within 250 ms, and `scrawl tune rust` prints the values to paste into `Tune::DEFAULT`. One animation clock (`motion::now()`, scaled by `timeScale`) for the coordinator's ages.

## Frame pacing (`src/native/pill/mod.rs`, `pacing.rs`)

The capsule lives in one fixed, transparent, non-activating panel (`PANEL_W` × `PANEL_H`, 640 × 92 pt, room for every state, the punch and the shader's drop shadow, `SHADOW_REACH`/`SHADOW_DROP`; every frame of every transition is checked to fit). It is placed once per display (`place_on`) and never moved or resized while it animates; the drawable size never changes. Its AppKit appear animation is off (`animationBehavior` None): ordering a utility panel in otherwise plays a window-server zoom and fade over the capsule's own entry. The panel takes the pointer only on the outline as drawn plus `HIT_SLOP` (4 pt) and lets everything else through (`ignoresMouseEvents`); the drawing thread checks the pointer against every frame it draws and sends only a change to the main thread, and the main thread's poll covers the capsule at rest.

The Metal capsule draws on its `CVDisplayLink`'s thread, never waiting for the main thread: the main thread only hands state over in `NativePill::sync` (presentation, clock, tune, time scale, pointer) under the `RenderState` lock. Each frame's springs advance by the time between the link's output timestamps (the refreshes the frames are shown on), so every frame moves by its own refresh interval and a missed refresh is caught up, not slowed down. No minimum or maximum step, no `now()` at draw time. The link follows the capsule's display (`set_current_cg_display`) and runs while anything visible moves, the recording dot's breathing and ripple and the processing spinner included (`PillModel::is_static`); it stops only while the capsule is hidden or truly still, and a `sync` that changes anything starts it again. Frames reach the screen in an explicit `CATransaction` (`presentsWithTransaction`), which keeps a 120 Hz compositor at 120 Hz; plain `present` held it to 60 and backed the drawable queue up. With Low Power Mode on, a ProMotion compositor runs at 60 Hz while the link ticks at 120, so only every other tick is drawn, on a fixed phase (`refresh_stride`); drawing both let the compositor pick one or the other and the motion stuttered. The glass style still draws on the main thread, since its `NSGlassEffectView` must move in the same transaction.

Each run of frames leaves one `pill frames:` line in `scrawl.log` (drawn, shown, replaced, worst gap, draw and lock-wait maxima), so real use leaves numbers. Measure with `SCRAWL_PILL_PACING=<file>` (window moves and pointer toggles as events; per frame: link tick, output time, draw time, spring clock, edges; per drawable: `presentedTime`) and `perf/pill/pacing.py <logs>` (dropped and doubled refreshes, worst gap, and the jitter between how far the springs advanced and how far the screen did). `SCRAWL_LAB_STALL_MS=<n>` blocks the lab's main thread for up to n ms per tick, standing in for the real app's work.

The Metal capsule (`src/native/pill/model.rs`) is a true capsule (radius = height / 2) around 32 pt chips inset 4 pt, with no dividers: items in a group sit 2 pt apart and groups (lead | chips | ✕) 6 pt apart. The selected mode is one highlight that slides between chips. Four `Tune` keys pick its options (`Tune::pill_opts`): `pill.origin` is where it enters and leaves from (0 centre scale, 1 drops open from the top edge, the default, 2 unrolls from the left end cap); `pill.anchor` 1 (default) lays the recording capsule's chips out where the picker puts them, so tapping Area or Window moves only the left wall and the highlight, and 0 keeps every state centred; `pill.blur` and `pill.shellBlur` are the content and edge blur on entry and exit (1.5 and 0 pt). Its springs come from the kit-motion palette and are tunable too: `pill.enter.*` (.25/0) and `pill.exit.*` (.2/0), `pill.morph.open.*` / `pill.morph.close.*` (.42/.16 and .38/.08, width only), and `pill.highlight.lead.*` / `pill.highlight.trail.*` (.18/0 and .25/0: the highlight's edge facing the slide leads, so it stretches toward the next chip and settles to its width; a hold-switcher key step is instant, .1/0). `pill.follow` (1.15) is how much longer content sliding with a morph takes than the walls when the capsule opens, so the shape leads and content follows it out; closing, content rides in on the walls' own spring. Neither ever lets content overtake a wall and get stopped against it. `pill.punch` (2 pt) is how far the reveal shoots past rest, on a curve over the zero-bounce entry spring (`punch_curve`); exits never shoot. Presets 7–12 are Centre, Unroll, Centred chips, 0.3.3 (its origin, blur and springs), Drop (zero bounce, no punch) and Punch (4 pt). Outline and rects are snapped edge by edge on a 1/128 pt grid, so an edge that isn't moving can't shimmer. Leaving items fade before a wall reaches them (`COLLAPSE_WAIT_S`, the look-ahead cap, which eases to nothing with the square of the time left); `nothing_vanishes_from_visible_opacity_in_one_frame_or_flickers` checks the last visible step at 60 and 120 Hz.

## SwiftUI capsule (`"pillRenderer": "swiftui"`, `helper/Pill.swift`, `src/native/swift_pill.rs`)

An alternative to the Metal capsule, off by default (`pillRenderer` in `config.json` or `SCRAWL_PILL_RENDERER=swiftui|metal`). `native::capsule::Capsule` opens it, starting `scrawl-helper pill` at launch, and falls back to Metal if the helper won't start or exits. Rust still decides what shows: each change goes over stdin as one JSON line with the resting layout from `model::layout_of` (capsule and items in panel points), the clock, the label, the style and the `Tune` springs (`swift_pill::state_message`). The helper reports `click`, `paste` (dragged off Paste), `hover` and `hidden` (the exit finished), which become the Metal capsule's `PillAction`s (`swift_pill::click_action`). The helper's one panel is fixed at 640 × 112 pt (the Metal panel's width and top, taller for the shadow and the Esc hint), never resized, and takes the mouse only over the capsule's shape.

How it moves is SwiftUI's: the capsule is a `Capsule` whose frame springs (`pill.morph.*`, by duration and bounce), the shape leading and the chips following (opening, sliding chips take `pill.follow` times the walls' duration, as on the Metal capsule; closing, they ride the walls' spring, and the wall waits 60 ms for leaving chips to start fading), as the Dynamic Island and DynamicNotchKit do it, chips come and go with an opacity + .96 scale + `pill.blur` transition (exits 15% shorter than `pill.exit`), the highlight is one `matchedGeometryEffect` shape on `pill.highlight.trail`, the timer uses `.numericText()`, and the entry runs `pill.enter` with `pill.punch` as a `CustomAnimation` (`punch_curve`) from `pill.origin`'s pose. `[[stitchable]]` shaders (`helper/PillShaders.metal`, compiled by `build.rs` and embedded as base64) add what SwiftUI shapes can't, each driven by an animation value: the rim lit from above with a soft inner glow along the edge, both swelling with each morph (a keyframe `energy` kick that settles with the morph spring); a 0.5 pt horizontal blur on the shell's walls (never the chips) while it morphs; the highlight lit like a glass key (brighter top, a catch on its upper rim); the recording dot's core, glow and ripple; and a slow light across "Processing…". `pill.highlight.lead` and `pill.shellBlur`'s timing don't map (one matched shape, no separate edge springs).

The animations run on the helper's main thread, which does nothing else, so the app's main thread can't touch them: with `SCRAWL_LAB_STALL_MS=40` the frames SwiftUI computes are as even as without. A stall in the helper itself (`SCRAWL_PILL_STALL_MS=<n>`, 80 ms into each state change) does freeze them for its length. `SCRAWL_PILL_TRACE=<file>` logs each frame SwiftUI computes for the capsule's edges, with its time.
