# Keyframes, transcription and the text agents read

Read this before changing `src/keyframes.rs`, `src/transcribe.rs`, `src/hex.rs`, `src/sketch.rs` (`summary.md`) or `src/render.rs` (frames and the contact sheet).

## Images and text

- **Images (`frames/*.png` and `contact-sheet.png`)**: Keep images purely visual. Each keyframe shows the screenshot, active red tldraw stroke(s) for that moment, click ripple/cursor, and a prominent high-contrast `#N M:SS` corner badge (white `#N` keycap + dark `M:SS` pill, top-left by default and dodging to top-right when top-left has ink/clicks). `contact-sheet.png` packs frames in time order (left→right, top→bottom) into aspect-ratio-justified rows separated by tight 6 px gutters on a dark canvas (no card borders or rounded frame masks, capped at 1600 px long edge / ~1.56 MP) with no rasterized transcript headers or caption boxes.
- **Text (`summary.md` & Clipboard)**: Put the transcript, `[#N · M:SS]` keyframe index, and absolute file paths (`Contact sheet:`, `frames/frame-XX.png`, `Video:`, `Bundle:`) in plain text (`NSPasteboardTypeString`) without repeating the transcript multiple times, so `⌘V` pastes cleanly into any terminal (Ghostty / OpenCode TUI) or GUI composer (OpenCode Desktop).

## Transcription (`src/transcribe.rs`, `src/hex.rs`)

Apple SpeechAnalyzer by default (measured word times). `"transcriber": "hex"` + `"hexModel"` in `~/.config/scrawl/config.json` (Settings › Recording, `scrawl hex status`, `--transcriber` on `reprocess`/`import`) uses Hex's local API v2 (discovery file `local-api.json`, bearer token, never logged) only when Hex answers `/health` and has a ready model (`installed && verified`; Scrawl never downloads). Hex gives text only, so each speech island (`audio::speech_islands`, clips merged to ≤ 24) is transcribed on its own and its words are spread over it by length: `source: "hex:<model>"`, `estimated: true`, which `keyframes.rs` reads to skip spoken cues. Any Hex failure falls back to Apple for that recording; Hex absent falls back silently (logged once). Settings reads `hex::cached_status` (a background poller), never the network.

## Keyframe selection (`src/keyframes.rs`)

- Only cut keyframes on real visual or input events (`Mark` strokes drawn while holding `⇧`, flags, `Click`/`Drag` inside the capture target or phone taps/swipes, `Laser` points, or settled `SceneChange` transitions)—never cut extra keyframes on speech pauses alone over a static screen.
- The one speech exception is a spoken cue with measured word timings (SpeechAnalyzer; never a Hex transcript, whose times are `estimated`): "now", "here", "look", "watch" snap to a transition that starts within −0.3 s…+1.2 s of the word, else take the frame 150 ms after it. A cue keyframe is dropped unless its image differs (scene diff ≥ 0.03) from every keyframe already chosen, so speech over an unchanged screen still yields one frame. Demonstratives and "see"/"there" are weak (they rank with ordinary scene changes) because they are usually filler.
- Budget (`keyframe_budget`): 6 up to 40 s, then one more per 20 s, at most 12 (each image costs a reading model ~1–1.5k tokens). Ordinary events fill only the first 6; the rest are for marks and coverage.
- Marks are never dropped. Marks drawn on the same screen share one keyframe at the last of them, which draws all their ink (`mark.frame` names it; `markId` is the first): the screen counts as the same while the luma grid around their ink, padded by 8%, changed in under 5% of its cells. A group closes at a click or drag, past 20 s, or past 12 strokes. Groups beyond the budget merge with a neighbour: same-screen pairs with the least ink first, else the most alike screens. Flags stay on their own. No other keyframe cuts into a multi-mark group's span. Auto-crop gives a group's ink to its keyframe's crop alone (`Input::pin_ink`).
- Coverage: after marks, a stretch longer than 100 words or 35 s gets a frame from inside it when one qualifies (a click, laser, settled scene change or novel cue); the lead-in before the first keyframe is its own stretch. Speech over an unchanged screen still yields one frame.
- Text per keyframe: each keyframe owns the words from the start of the clause in progress at its anchor (action start, transition start, or cue word) until the next keyframe's start. Clauses break at clause punctuation, recognizer segment starts, or a ≥1.5 s pause. Words before the first keyframe belong to it.

## Experiment `agentContext` (`src/agent_context.rs`)

Off by default (see Experiments in `AGENTS.md`). With it on, `sketch::summary_markdown` hands each keyframe to `agent_context::keyframe_texts`, which recovers the keyframe's timed words from its `said` and breaks them up by what happened while they were said: marks (`✎ M:SS <description>`), clicks, drags (moves under 0.01 are a held click's jitter and are left out), scrolls, shortcuts, and repeats of one item within 3 s folded into `×N` (`pressed ⌘T ×3`, a click on one spot), ordinary windows opening and closing (layer 0, by app name; titles never leave `scrawl.json`), app switches by app name, ScrawlKit app events and context changes. Speech before the first of these stays on the bullet line; each mark, or run of events with no speech between them, starts an indented line followed by the words said until the next one. An item's place in the speech moves back to the start of its clause if that began within 3 s, else forward past the clause's last word if it ends within 1.2 s, so a clause isn't cut in half. Events belong to the keyframe whose stretch they fall in (a stretch starts at the keyframe's first word, first mark, own moment or, for a click keyframe, its click up to 1.5 s before); marks always belong to their own keyframe. Without word times (a segment-only transcript) the speech stays whole and the items follow it.

When a keyframe draws two or more marks they are numbered ①②③ in drawing order (`sketch::marks_on`), in the text and on the image: `render::draw_mark_numbers` puts a small dark, white-ringed disc with the number just up and left of where each mark's first stroke began. Single-mark keyframes stay unnumbered.

A scene-change keyframe says where the screen changed: `[changed: x 0.70–0.96, y 0.02–0.12; …]` from `autocrop.changes` for that keyframe, boxes merged when they touch, the largest two shown with a count of the rest, or `[changed: the whole screen]` when the largest covers 90%.

With `agentContext` on when a Mac capture starts (not the iPhone mirror, and only with Accessibility granted), `crate::ax` looks up what each click and ⇧ stroke lands on in the Accessibility tree: the element at the mouse-down point, and for a stroke the middle of its box (just above a flat stroke, since an underline sits under what it marks). A click on a button's text or icon is named by the button (up to two parents). It reads the role description, a label (title, description, value, placeholder), `AXIdentifier` or `AXDOMIdentifier`, and the file name of the window's `AXDocument`; never a window title, never the value of a secure or other input field, and every string cut to one line of 60 characters. Lookups run one at a time on the `scrawl-ax` thread with a 0.1 s messaging timeout and a 250 ms budget each; lookups that hit Scrawl's own windows retry on the app whose window is under the point. `finish_session` waits up to 400 ms for running lookups and puts the results on `Click.role` / `Click.label` (`“New Tab” #tab-new in main.rs`), `Drag.label` and `Stroke.on` (`button “Area”`, which `Mark.on` and the mark's description pick up). Results of an earlier capture are dropped (`Prober::begin`).

Dev pages that load `/sdk.js` add what only the page knows (`src/page.rs`, `src/sdk.js`). With `agentContext` on, `/web/register` answers `"context": true` to loopback pages, and the script then hooks in once: the element under each `pointerdown` and under ⇧ mouse moves (every 250 ms) as tag, id, two classes, a label (`aria-label`, `title`, `alt`, `data-testid`, else up to 60 characters of text; for form fields only the placeholder or name, nothing for password fields, never a typed value), and the component and source `file:line` from React's fiber (`_debugSource`, or the first project frame of `_debugStack`) or a locator attribute (`data-source-loc`, `data-inspector-relative-path`/`-line`, `data-sentry-component`/`-source-file`); `<video>`/`<audio>` seeks, plays and pauses (file name, time, duration); `console.error`, uncaught errors and unhandled rejections (message and first project frame); and fetch/XHR requests that fail or answer 400+ (method, path without query, status, duration; no bodies, headers or cookies). It batches them to `POST /web/context`, which takes loopback pages from this Mac only, token or not, and drops them when the experiment is off. The app keeps 3 minutes per tab (300 events, 16 tabs) and cuts every string again. When a capture finishes (`finish_session`, not the iPhone mirror), `page::apply` takes the window's events from tabs that were clicked, marked or focused then: a page click within 400 ms of a Mac click or drag replaces its label (`<button.tab-new> “New tab” in BrowserHeader (src/BrowserHeader.tsx:88)`), the most common element under a stroke's ⇧ moves becomes its `on`, and errors, failed requests and video playback become app events (`console error: …`, `GET /api/tabs → 404`, `video clips.mp4 scrubbed to 0:04 / 0:11`, a scrub's seeks folded into its last). The agentContext summary shows a Mac capture's app events without the `app:` prefix.

## Experiment `liveTranscribe` (`src/live_transcribe.rs`, `helper/LiveTranscribe.swift`)

Off by default (see Experiments in `AGENTS.md`). With it on and Apple Speech the transcriber, `AudioRecorder::start` also starts `scrawl-helper transcribe-stream` (one SpeechAnalyzer, finalized results only; it never downloads a model, so one that isn't installed fails at once) and a thread that feeds it the recorder's 16 kHz buffer as `audio::pcm16` every 160 ms. `RecordedAudio.live` carries it to `transcribe::transcribe`, which sends the samples it hasn't had from the finished WAV, closes its input and waits up to 6 s for the tail; the helper must report exactly the WAV's sample count. Word times are therefore the WAV's own, and match one SpeechAnalyzer pass over `audio.wav` word for word and millisecond for millisecond (the batch path splits recordings over 22 s at silences and transcribes the pieces in parallel, so long recordings can differ from it at the cuts). Any failure is logged (`Live transcription failed, …`) and the WAV is transcribed as before; Hex, iPhone and imported recordings never stream. `scrawl reprocess B --out O --bench 1 --live-speed 1` replays a bundle's audio into it in real time and logs `Live transcription: N words, … tail N ms` and the finish timings.

To compare a summary experiment on real captures, `scripts/agent-eval.sh [--experiment NAME] [--out DIR] [bundle…]` reprocesses each bundle with and without it (Kit's own `experiments` removed from the baseline's config) and writes `before.md`, `after.md`, both contact sheets and frames per bundle, and an `index.md`, by default to `~/code/home/artifacts/scrawl-context/`. Both summaries come from the baseline run's transcript. What a capture recorded live (Accessibility lookups, page events) is kept as recorded, so those only show on captures made with the experiment on.
